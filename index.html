Seeing Autumn from a Different Point of View

Our Artistic Journey

This section of the Autumn Impressions Curriculum is called FALL Imprints. It follows the children's developing relationship with trees, from the youngest child's handprint to an older student's chosen pathway through an autumn landscape.

Each level builds on familiar movements and observations while introducing new ways of seeing and expressing.
