NATURE in our HANDS

Creating Art Through Natural Materials

Our Artistic Journey

NATURE in our HANDS invites students to discover the artistic possibilities of natural materials while developing an appreciation for their textures, forms and individual characteristics.

Toddlers and PreCASA children begin with lacing activities using wooden log slices, developing hand-eye coordination, concentration and purposeful movement.

CASA students progress to constructing owls from natural wood, introducing three-dimensional form and the symbolic significance of the owl as a messenger of knowledge.

Elementary students expand their exploration through natural-material collage, assemblage and increasingly intentional compositions.

By Grades 7 and 8, students consider the relationship between natural materials, artistic expression and personal meaning, transforming simple elements of nature into thoughtful works of art.

TODDLERS

Exploring Wood through Lacing

Toddlers explore the texture of natural wood discs with prepared holes, practising threading raffia lace through openings and pulling it through.

The focus is on sensory exploration, hand coordination and purposeful movement. Through holding, threading and pulling, children develop confidence, independence and coordination through repetition.

PRECASA

Autumn Threads

PreCASA children explore natural wood discs using red, yellow and orange raffia laces, threading through holes and crossing laces to create overlapping designs.

The focus is on sensory exploration, hand coordination and purposeful movement. Children discover how laces connect holes and form shapes while developing independence and making creative choices.

casa 1

A Community of Owls

CASA 1 students create owls on natural wood, arranging recognisable features to give each owl its own character.

The focus is on recognising form, creative expression and community belonging. Displayed together, the owls represent the mixed-age CASA environment, with larger CASA 3 owls symbolising the older students’ growing role as mentors.

casa 2

A Community of Owls

CASA 2 students create medium owls on wood, exploring placement, proportion and details to give each owl its own character.

The focus is on recognising form, creative expression and community belonging. Together, the owls represent the mixed-age CASA environment, with larger CASA 2 owls symbolising the older students’ growing role as mentors.

casa 3

A Community of Owls

CASA 3 students create large owls on natural wood, exploring individual features while representing growing knowledge, independence and responsibility.

The focus is on recognising form, creative expression and community belonging. Together, the owls represent the mixed-age CASA environment, with larger CASA 3 owls symbolising their role as mentors to younger students.

GRADE 1

Nature’s First Arrangement

Grade 1 students create collages using up to two types of natural materials, such as acorns, pinecones, dried leaves or wheat, arranging them freely.

The focus is on texture, shape and creative exploration. Students handle, compare and reposition objects before securing them, discovering how natural materials can form representational or abstract compositions.

GRADE 2

Autumn in Balance

Grade 2 students create collages using three types of natural materials, choosing their own subjects and experimenting with different arrangements.

The focus is on grouping, repetition, contrast and balance. Students explore how different textures and shapes work together, developing compositions while maintaining freedom to experiment and make creative choices.

GRADE 3

A Landscape of Texture

Grade 3 students create collages using four types of natural materials, exploring overlapping, clustering and spreading objects across their chosen compositions.

The focus is on placement, layering, balance and texture. Students discover how materials combine through exploration, creating representational or abstract arrangements while developing independence.

GRADE 4

One Element, One Expression

Grade 4 students create nature-inspired artwork highlighting one of the seven elements of art: line, shape, form, space, value, colour or texture.

The focus is on intentional material choices and visual expression. Students explore how twigs, bark or contrasting colours communicate artistic ideas, explaining how their chosen element appears in their composition.

GRADE 5

Elements in Conversation

Grade 5 students create nature-inspired artwork highlighting two of the seven elements of art: line, shape, form, space, value, colour or texture.

The focus is on combining visual elements to strengthen composition. Students explore how materials such as twigs, bark and leaves express different elements, making purposeful choices to show how they work together.

GRADE 6

Elements in Harmony

Grade 6 students create nature-inspired artwork highlighting four of the seven elements of art: line, shape, form, space, value, colour or texture.

The focus is on visual unity, composition and focal points. Students plan how their chosen elements work together, guiding the viewer’s eye through their artwork while explaining how each element contributes.

GRADE 7

Path through Nature

Grade 7 students create natural relief landscapes using twigs, bark, leaves and seed heads to form pathways that curve, narrow or disappear.

The focus is on direction, depth and visual movement. Students vary material size, height and density to suggest distance while exploring how pathways express discovery, uncertainty and personal choices.

GRADE 8

A Place to Pause

Grade 8 students create natural compositions using twigs, bark and textures to surround an intentionally open space, making the void their focal point.

The focus is on negative space, composition and personal reflection. Students explore how shape, placement and restraint create calm, connecting their artwork to autumn’s changes and the importance of reflection.
