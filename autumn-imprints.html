FALL Imprints

Exploring Autumn Through Colour, Form and Perspective

Our Artistic Journey

FALL Imprints celebrates the beauty of autumn through colour, texture, shape and perspective.

Beginning with Toddlers exploring handprints and primary colours, the work progresses through PreCASA colour mixing and CASA folk art trees, introducing line, pattern and composition.

Elementary students develop their understanding of perspective by drawing trees from the unusual viewpoint of looking upward through autumn foliage.

As students advance, their work incorporates multiple viewpoints, spatial relationships, light, shading and increasingly sophisticated compositions.

By Grades 7 and 8, autumn becomes a source of personal reflection, with students using colour, imagery and artistic expression to communicate deeper meaning.

Toddlers

The First Impression

This is a handprint impression of the child’s RH and LH, with thumbs crossing. The child will glue a precut tree trunk with branches onto the canvas.

They will choose the same or different colours for their hands and name their colour choices. Using a stamp, they will add “hand leaves” at the bottom of the trunk.

Colours: Red | Yellow | Orange
Project Duration: 4 Days

PreCASA

A Tree Through Touch

This is a handprint impression of the child’s dominant hand and fingerprints. The child will trace their hand and lower arm onto brown construction paper and cut it out if able.

They will trace a large circle on the canvas and fill it with fingertip “paint dabs” in autumn colours. Fingerprints will be added at the bottom of the trunk as fallen leaves.

Colours: Red | Yellow | Orange | Brown | Colour Mixing
Project Duration: 4 Days

CASA 1

Folk Art Trees

Children create “Folk Art” trees using line art. They will practise drawing trees using vertical, horizontal, diagonal and curved lines, introducing zigzags and gentle waves.

Each tree design will be practised in their Sketch Book, where they will label the different lines. They will then choose three trees to transfer onto canvas and paint.

Colours: Seasonal Colour Choices

CASA 2

Folk Art Trees

Children create “Folk Art” trees using line art. Building on CASA 1, they will practise drawing trees using wavy lines, zigzags, spirals, loops and parallel lines.

Each tree design will be practised in their Sketch Book, where they will label the different lines. They will then choose three trees to transfer onto canvas and paint.

Colours: Seasonal Colour Choices

CASA 3

Folk Art Trees

Children create “Folk Art” trees using line art. Building on earlier skills, they explore contour, radiating and crosshatched lines, introducing simple perspective and overlapping forms.

They will practise and label new tree designs in their Sketch Book before creating a composition to transfer onto canvas and paint.

Colours: Seasonal Colour Choices

Grade 1

One Tree, One View

Students draw a single autumn tree from the perspective of lying beneath it and looking upward. The trunk rises toward the sky, with branches extending outward through autumn foliage.

The focus is on viewpoint, upward direction, proportions and autumn colour. They will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.

Grade 2

Two Trees, Two Perspectives

Students introduce a second tree, exploring how both trees appear from the viewer’s position and relate to one another. Overlapping branches and differences in size and placement help establish depth.

The focus is on spatial relationships, scale, overlapping forms and composition, developing a stronger understanding of perspective.

Grade 3

Three Trees and the Sun

Building on Grade 2, students introduce a third tree, with the sun becoming the central focal point shining through the autumn foliage. Tree placement directs the viewer’s attention toward the light.

The focus is on focal points, three-tree composition, light, overlapping forms and perspective.

Grade 4

Surrounded by Autumn

The perspective expands to include trees on every side, creating the impression of being surrounded by an autumn forest. Sunlight filters through the branches, blending seasonal colours.

The focus is on surrounding perspective, background, depth and composition. Students will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.

Grade 5

Light, Shadow and Definition

Building on Grade 4, students add greater definition to tree trunks and branches. Sunlight filtering through the foliage introduces directional shading, contrast and a stronger sense of atmosphere.

The focus is on shading, light source, texture and depth. Students will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.

Grade 6

The Birch Forest

Students create a dense birch forest using advanced line drawing to define the distinctive bark, trunks and branches. They combine perspective, foliage, light and shading to develop depth.

The focus is on advanced line work, bark texture, layered perspective and contrast. Students will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.

Grade 7

The Road Less Travelled

Students shift their viewpoint forward, creating an autumn road lined with trees. Linear perspective and a vanishing point establish distance, while colour and light express the meaning of following one's own path.

The focus is on perspective, atmospheric colour, symbolism and emotional expression. Students will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.

Grade 8

Light, Shadow and Definition

Students build on the Grade 4 composition, adding greater definition to tree trunks and branches. Sunlight filtering through the foliage introduces directional shading, contrast and a stronger sense of atmosphere.

The focus is on shading, light source, texture and depth. Students will practise the drawing in their Sketch Book before transferring it onto canvas.
