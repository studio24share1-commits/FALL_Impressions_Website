LAYERS of AUTUMN

Exploring Nature Through Leaf Impressions

Our Artistic Journey

LAYERS of AUTUMN explores the natural beauty and individuality of leaves through a progressive study of printmaking, colour and composition.

Toddlers begin with a single leaf impression, discovering the relationship between pressing and lifting, while PreCASA children explore repetition and placement.

CASA students investigate differences in leaf shapes, overlapping forms and contrasting colours.

Through the Elementary years, students develop increasingly complex compositions, exploring layering, movement, spatial relationships, light and the patterns found in nature.

By Grades 7 and 8, leaf impressions become a more sophisticated artistic language, encouraging students to communicate personal interpretations of autumn, transformation and change.

TODDLERS

One Little Leaf

Toddlers explore a real leaf through touch and observation before using paint to create a single leaf impression on paper.

The focus is on purposeful movement, hand–eye coordination and cause and effect. Through pressing and lifting, children discover the marks they create while learning vocabulary for materials, colours and actions.

PRECASA

A Leaf, Again

PreCASA children explore the shape and texture of a leaf before using paint to create two impressions of the same leaf on paper.

The focus is on purposeful movement, hand–eye coordination, repetition and independence. Through pressing and lifting, children discover how one leaf creates multiple impressions while exploring placement.

CASA 1

Every Leaf is Different

CASA 1 students explore leaves of different shapes, sizes and details, practising leaf stencilling on paper before transferring their designs to canvas with paint.

The focus is on visual discrimination, observation and recognising similarities and differences. Students develop awareness of leaf shapes and details while practising careful placement and painting techniques.

CASA 2

Leaves Together

CASA 2 students explore two different leaf shapes, practising stencilling on paper before transferring their designs to canvas with paint. They arrange impressions beside or overlapping one another.

The focus is on spatial awareness, composition and placement. Students discover how overlapping creates depth and how positioning leaves changes their artwork.

CASA 3

Autumn Aglow

CASA 3 students explore autumn colours, practising leaf stencilling on paper before transferring their designs to black canvas. They arrange and overlap impressions in reds, oranges, yellows and gold.

The focus is on colour contrast, composition and independent planning. Students discover how dark spaces define leaf shapes and make vibrant colours stand out.

GRADE 1

Leaf upon Leaf

Grade 1 students practise leaf stencilling on paper before transferring repeated impressions of the same leaf to canvas, allowing some to overlap.

The focus is on layering, depth and composition. Students explore how placement and colour create foreground and background while developing planning, controlled printing and thoughtful colour choices.

GRADE 2

Autumn Gathering

Grade 2 students practise leaf stencilling on paper before transferring layered impressions to canvas, exploring colour, direction and placement across a larger space.

The focus is on composition, balance and spatial awareness. Students explore how overlapping leaves and visible background guide the viewer’s eye while developing planning and thoughtful artistic choices.

GRADE 3

A Carpet of Leaves

Grade 3 students practise designs in sketchbooks before transferring overlapping leaf impressions to canvas, varying direction, placement and autumn colours.

The focus is on unity, visual texture and composition. Students explore how repeated shapes create the appearance of fallen leaves covering the ground while preserving individual details and creating artwork.

GRADE 4

Autumn in Colour

Grade 4 students practise designs in sketchbooks before transferring layered leaf impressions to canvas, arranging autumn colours from yellow through orange to red.

The focus is on colour relationships, rhythm and visual unity. Students explore how overlapping colours and gradual transitions guide the viewer’s eye while developing intentional sequencing and composition.

GRADE 5

Leaves in Motion

Grade 5 students practise designs in sketchbooks before transferring leaf impressions to canvas, arranging them along curves and spirals to suggest wind.

The focus is on movement, visual rhythm and composition. Students vary direction, spacing and overlapping colours to create swirling clusters and drifting leaves, exploring how still artwork communicates motion.

GRADE 6

Light of the Forest Floor

Grade 6 students create layered leaf impressions, beginning with darker colours before adding lighter shades to suggest sunlight across a forest floor.

The focus is on value, depth and emphasis. Students explore how overlapping leaves, shadows and brighter patches create atmosphere, guide the viewer’s eye and establish a focal point.

GRADE 7

Between the Leaves

Grade 7 students arrange overlapping leaf impressions, deliberately leaving areas of the background visible to explore the shapes formed between leaves.

The focus is on positive and negative space, balance and composition. Students explore how open spaces guide the viewer’s eye, creating visual balance while giving unprinted areas equal importance to leaf shapes.

GRADE 8

Impressions of Change

Grade 8 students combine printing and stencilling, using layered leaves, colour and open space to express personal reflections on autumn and change.

The focus is on symbolism, composition and personal expression. Students explore how gathering, drifting or fading leaves convey growth and transition, explaining their artistic choices through a short reflection.
